- `candidate` - identified, not contacted yet
- `contacted` - outreach sent
- `interested` - positive reply, awaiting details
- `vetted` - identity and independence checked
- `scheduled` - assigned a contribution slot
- `completed` - contribution accepted
- `dropped` - removed from slate
